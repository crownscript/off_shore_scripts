QUESTION: WHAT IS THE AMERICAN COALITION FOR POLICE AND SHERIFFS PAC?
RESPONSE: THE AMERICAN COALITION FOR POLICE AND SHERIFFS PAC IS A POLITICAL COMMITTEE WHOS MISSION IS TO PROVIDE CRITICAL SUPPORT TO LAW ENFORCEMENT BY ADVOCATING FOR LEGISLATION THAT WILL PROTECT OUR OFFICERS RIGHTS AND PROVIDE BENEFITS TO OFFICERS KILLED OR INJURED IN THE LINE OF DUTY.

QUESTION: ARE YOU A MEMBER OF THE ORGANIZATION?
RESPONSE: NO, I WORK FOR A COMPANY HIRED TO CONDUCT THESE CALLS FOR THE ASSOCIATION.

QUESTION: IS THIS A DEMOCRAT OR REPUBLICAN ORGANIZATION?
RESPONSE: THIS ORGANIZATION IS NOT DEMOCRATIC OR REPUBLICAN; IT'S A BIPARTISAN ORGANIZATION FOCUSING ON THE NEEDS OF LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS.

QUESTION: WHO IS THE HEAD OF THIS ORGANIZATION?
RESPONSE: THIS COMMITTEE WAS ORGANIZED AND MANAGED BY A GROUP OF CITIZENS CONCERNED FOR THE DETERIORATING SUPPORT FOR LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS.

QUESTION: WHAT IS THE TAX STATUS OF THIS ORGANIZATION?
RESPONSE: THE ASSOCIATION IS A 527 ORGANIZATION UNDER THE IRS TAX CODE. DUE TO ITS PARTICULAR TAX STATUS, CONTRIBUTIONS ARE NOT TAX DEDUCTIBLE.

QUESTION: WHERE IS THE ORGANIZATION LOCATED?
RESPONSE: THE POLITICAL COMMITTEE IS LOCATED IN WASHINGTON DC.

QUESTION: DO THEY HAVE A WEBSITE?
RESPONSE: YOU CAN VISIT THE ASSOCIATION ON THE WEB AT www.americanpolice.org
